The inputs argue

A remix of Misty (CHI 2024). Before Misty blends an example into your UI, the two argue about it in gibberish voices, and you side with or against each point. The blend follows your rulings.
This page is a self-contained demo: the debate is scripted and the blend is built in the page, so no model or server is needed. In the remix, Misty writes a new debate for each blend and the model makes the blend. Turn your sound on.
